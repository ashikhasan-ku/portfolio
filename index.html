How I work

Clarity before speed

A shared understanding of the problem saves more time than a fast start. I ask the awkward questions in week one.

Make the risk visible

Assumptions, dependencies and open decisions live on the page where everyone can see them, with an owner and a date.

Speak both languages

A maths degree and years in analytics mean I can talk data models with engineers and outcomes with business owners.

Report decisions, not activity

Good status updates say what changed, what is blocked and what someone needs to decide.
