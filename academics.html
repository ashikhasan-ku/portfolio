Maths at work

Where the maths shows up at work

Three habits from a mathematics degree that I use in every project.

Estimation and forecasting

Break big unknowns into small, countable parts, then add them up honestly. PERT is a weighted average of optimistic, likely and pessimistic.

80% accuracy on budget and timeline forecasts for Yamaha Thailand’s data warehouse.

Probability and risk

Every plan is a bet. A RAID log writes the odds down so the team can act on them early.

Regular RAID reviews and contingency planning at Daraz, where 90% of projects finished on time.

Models and data

Comfortable with data models, KPIs and SQL, so conversations with data teams start from the same page.

30+ BI solutions delivered across 25 countries for PIMCO.
