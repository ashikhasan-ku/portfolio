Brain Station 23Telco, web, cloud, learning platforms, SaaS2026 to now

From the first client meeting to the final invoice

Why it mattered

In a services company, a weak scope becomes a loss-making project. The plan, price and team have to be right before anyone writes code, and still be right at sign-off.

My role

I own the whole lifecycle: finding the opportunity, discovery, WBS and estimates, the proposal, resourcing and cost control, then delivery through to close.

Hunt

Spot SaaS and custom-build opportunities worth pursuing, then shape them into products.

Shaping two SaaS products: an agentic-AI garment-sector platform tracking each order from enquiry to payment, and a school parent app on subscription pricing.

Discover

Run the first client meetings and turn what people say into requirements an engineer can estimate.

Discovery sessions for training, learning and website clients, captured as feature lists and meeting minutes.

Scope and estimate

Build a feature-level WBS where every row is an actor, an action and a result, so each item has a clear definition of done. Estimate with PERT and analogous methods, and check feasibility with engineering.

Estimates reviewed with technical leads before they reach the client.

Propose

Write the technical and commercial proposal, and choose a pricing model that fits the client.

Proposals across telco, cybersecurity, website design, learning platforms, enterprise collaboration, mobile apps and order management, priced as fixed fee, SaaS subscription or revenue share.

Plan

Allocate people, set milestones and track cost performance (CPI) so the plan holds once work starts.

Resource allocation and CPI tracking across concurrent engagements.

Deliver

Keep one line of communication with the client, manage change requests and protect quality.

A three-sprint telco proof of concept, website change requests, and a digital experience website build now under way.

Close

Get acceptance, hand over cleanly and close the commercials.

A QA and security audit engagement run from kickoff to final invoice, with security test cases delivered as a separate QA report.

Daraz (Alibaba Group)Logistics network2026

Closing 14 hubs without breaking sellers’ orders

Why it mattered

Analysis showed some hubs were not worth keeping. Every seller in them had to move to another hub without losing orders or stock.

My role

I ran the seller migration and the communication between the teams involved.

What I did

  • Built the trackers for every impacted seller
  • Ran each move as pause, shift, confirm, reactivate, with stock and approval checks at every step
  • Managed communication between the teams involved

Result
